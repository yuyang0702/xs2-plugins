# Natural prose guidance

Reviewed 2026-11-02. The existing writer, specialist, voice, editor and reader resources use a conservative, original-first approach. Facts, author intent and this book's adopted style take precedence over generic editing patterns. A direct feeling, ordinary repetition, a long sentence or a quiet scene is not a defect by itself.

The guidance is an independent adaptation of the following editorial methods. No upstream scripts, model weights or complete skill packages are bundled or executed.

| Source | Reviewed revision | Used | Deliberately not adopted |

These projects provide methods, not evidence that this plugin's output is human-written or that an AI detector will return any particular score. The local scene comparison rejected several attractive-looking rewrites for factual regressions. Existing manuscripts and model preferences are not rewritten during resource updates.

## Runtime contracts

- Existing JSON output keys, writer slot keys, workflow variable bindings and source ownership remain intact.
- Instructions such as forbidden events or access restrictions are internal constraints, not new dialogue or narration.
- Reviews require a verifiable quotation and an actual reading impact. Rhythm statistics and word lists cannot automatically trigger a rewrite.
- Unclear major changes remain for author review. No new evidence, background, crisis or ending may be invented to fix wording.
- Customized workflows are preserved. Updating an older local definition requires a backed-up, exact-clause change; never replace the entire custom definition or model preferences.
